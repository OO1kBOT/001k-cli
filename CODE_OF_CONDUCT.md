# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone. We pledge to act and
interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and
healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include demonstrating empathy and kindness, being
respectful of differing opinions, giving and gracefully accepting constructive
feedback, and focusing on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behavior
includes harassment, trolling or insulting comments, publishing others' private
information, and other conduct which could reasonably be considered
inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the maintainers at **conduct@001k.bot**. All complaints will be
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
